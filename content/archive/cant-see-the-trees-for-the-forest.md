Coming to Canada from the Netherlands I wanted to reconnect with *the* Nature. More than once people would ask, “how do you do nature?”—as if it were a sport. The works collect fragments from a failed attempt to find a singular, unspoiled nature free from cultural packaging.

## Souvenir

Three pieces of foraged old-growth red-cedar (gathered after volunteering on a boardwalk construction in the Wah-nuh-jus—Hilthoois Tribal Park). To pass customs as “clean,” woodworker Justin Davids at Yew Woodshop finished them as little as possible while still appearing treated—the sticks needed to pass both as natural and as artefacts.

## Metallic bird chirps

A phone looping birdsong that retweets Vilém Flusser’s essay *The Cedar* every five minutes, alongside text and animation.

## Backdrop

The old-growth forest T'l'oqwxwat on Pacheedaht territory outside Port Renfrew, Vancouver Island, was nicknamed “Avatar Grove” in 2009 to attract tourists and protect it from logging. A wallpaper loops footage of the grove processed through an AI to fuse it with the CGI palette of the blockbuster film.

## Postcard

Postcards with tree marking tape and ink, circulating a fragment of the forest as a portable relic.
