This publication gathers practitioners from archaeology, biology, art, and law to explore methods of communicating with the other-than-human.

Texts by MT Ursch, Sami Hammana, Arvid & Marie, Claire Tio, Maaike van Papeveld, Niekolaas Johannes Lekkerkerk, Menno Schilthuizen, Marjolijn Kok, Lisa Velema, Quentin Lazzareschi, Rob Stone, Josué Amador, and Niina Rätsep. Presented at Print Room Rotterdam.
