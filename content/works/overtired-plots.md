Developed in collaboration with George Ridgway during a residency at Mustarinda, this video positions itself between two scales of the forest: the surrounding old-growth stands, among the last of their kind in Finland, and LUMI, the European Union supercomputer built to model digital twins of the world’s forests and their biodiversity.

The residency and production were supported by the Swedish Arts Grants Committee and the Swedish-Finnish Cultural Foundation.
