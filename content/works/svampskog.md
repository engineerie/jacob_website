Skogssvamp (forest mushroom) is a web application that visualises what the presence and distribution of mushrooms in different forest stands might look like. The project highlights the largely hidden diversity of mycorrhizal fungi in Swedish forests and lets users test how management choices could shift the species composition of soil fungi.

The work is a collaboration between Jacob Bertilsson and Anders Dahlberg with a two-year project grant from Skogssällskapet, based at the Swedish University of Agricultural Sciences. Early presentations were given at the Oxford Real Farming Conference (2023) and at the Mustarinda Association (2023).
