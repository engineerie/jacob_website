In 2009, to protect the old-growth forest T'l'oqwxwat (Pacheedaht territory) from logging and to attract tourists, a local campaign rebranded it after James Cameron’s *Avatar*, calling it “Avatar Grove.”

Footage from the forest is processed through an AI to transpose the film’s visual style, overlaid with a transcribed conversation between two local environmentalists discussing alternative models to park conservation. The commercial AI repeatedly mishears “tribal park” as “travel park.” Voices in the video belong to Emily Hoffpauir and Torrance Coste from the Wilderness Committee.
